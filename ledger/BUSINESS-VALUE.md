# Ledger — Business Value

## What It Does

Simple financial system for small businesses. Tells you exactly what's profitable, what's costing you money, and where your cash is going.

## Why It Matters

**Stop guessing about money.**
- See entire financial picture in one place
- Know which products/services actually make money
- Understand real profit margins
- Track cash flow, not just revenue

**Never run out of stock.**
- Automatic reorder alerts based on actual sales
- Know exactly how much to order to minimize costs
- See orders in transit before they arrive

**Make better decisions.**
- Know your break-even point
- See contribution margin per sale
- Understand unit economics
- Forecast revenue based on history

**Save time.**
- Automatic financial statements (Income, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet)
- Track recurring revenue and expenses automatically
- Import/export data easily
- Works on any device — phone, tablet, desktop

**Grow past "just you."**
- Bring on manager or staff without handing over margins
- Staff can log sales and count cash — never see cost prices
- Every action enforced by database, not just hidden buttons

## Who It's For

Solo entrepreneurs and small businesses (1-10 employees) selling products, services, or both. Professional financial tracking without hiring an accountant.

## The Bottom Line

Better financial decisions through better financial visibility.
