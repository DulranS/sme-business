# Ledger — Business Value Overview

## What Ledger Does for Your Business

Ledger is a complete financial management system designed specifically for solo and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) that sell products, services, or both. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets and manual calculations with a unified platform that tracks every dollar flowing through your business.

## Core Business Benefits

### **Complete Financial Visibility**
- See your entire business financial picture in one place
- Real-time dashboard with revenue, costs, inventory value, and profit metrics
- Monthly profit & loss statements that show exactly where money comes from and where it goes
- Three standard financial statements (Income, Cash Flow, Balance Sheet) generated automatically

### **Inventory Intelligence**
- **Never run out of stock**: Automatic reorder alerts based on your actual sales velocity and supplier lead times
- **Optimal ordering**: Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) calculations tell you exactly how much to order to minimize costs
- **Accurate costing**: Weighted Average Cost engine ensures your cost of goods sold reflects real purchase prices
- **Track committed spend**: See wholesale orders in transit before they arrive, so you know what's coming

### **Profitability Insights**
- **Know your best performers**: Item-level profitability shows which products/services make the most money
- **Understand unit economics**: See contribution margin per sale after accounting for variable costs
- **Pricing power signals**: Margin categorization (thin/moderate/healthy/strong) helps identify pricing opportunities
- **Break-even clarity**: Know exactly how much revenue you need to cover overhead costs

### **Cash Flow Management**
- **See cash vs. profit**: Cash Flow Statement shows actual money movement, not just accrual accounting
- **Plan ahead**: Revenue forecasting based on your historical trends helps you anticipate cash needs
- **Track capital separately**: Owner investments, reinvestments, and withdrawals are tracked outside operating P&L
- **Loan management**: Automatic amortization schedules show principal vs. interest payments

### **Operational Efficiency**
- **Unified catalog**: Products and services in one system with consistent costing
- **Automated payroll**: Employee records automatically generate recurring payroll expenses
- **Recurring revenue/expenses**: Track subscriptions, retainers, and recurring costs with automatic monthly normalization
- **MRR calculation**: Monthly Recurring Revenue computed automatically for subscription-based businesses

### **Data Control & Portability**
- **Your data, your way**: CSV export for every entity type — you own your data
- **Bulk imports**: Load products, purchases, sales, expenses, and employees from CSV with validation
- **Offline capability**: Works offline with local caching, syncs when connection returns
- **Private & secure**: Firebase authentication keeps your financial data accessible only to you

## Who This Is For

**Perfect for:**
- Solo entrepreneurs running product businesses
- Service providers who need to track time/materials costs
- Hybrid businesses (products + services)
- Small retailers with wholesale purchasing
- Businesses with 1-10 employees
- Anyone who needs professional-grade financial tracking without an accountant

**Not designed for:**
- Multi-tenant team collaboration
- Complex multi-entity corporate structures
- Industries requiring specialized accounting (construction, manufacturing with work-in-progress, etc.)

## The Bottom Line

Ledger gives you the financial intelligence of a much larger business without the complexity or cost. You'll know exactly what's profitable, what needs attention, and where your cash is going — all in one system that works the way you work.

**Key outcome**: Better financial decisions through better financial visibility.
